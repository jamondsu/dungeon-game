    Fire:
    - main attack (fireball) cd: 2s
        - fires a fireball in the direction of the mouse relative to the player position
    - special passive ability
        - burn: attacks inflict burn stacks on the enemy. these stacks disappear when no more consecutive attacks are inflicted on the enemy in 4 seconds. there are 4 degrees of burn
            - 1st degree: small damage and only 2 ticks, every 3 seconds, requires 0 stacks
            - 2nd degree: mediocre damage and 4 ticks, every 2 seconds, requires 3 stacks
            - 3rd degree: high damage and 4 ticks, every second, requires 6 stacks
            - 4th degree: very high damage and 4 ticks, every half second, requires 12 stacks
            - important note: when progressing to a higher degree, the previous remaining ticks of the lower burn are dissolved and only the higher tier burn will apply
            - another important note: when inflicting a burn on a certain degree, it will only refresh the burn of the the degree it is currently in. so scorching an enemy that is already in degree 2 isnt as worth it as scorching an enemy that will move from degree 1 to 2
    - ultimate: scorch cd: 1m
        - in a small radius around your mouse pointer, apply 1 burn stack every 0.5 seconds to all enemies in that radius, this lasts for 3 seconds (so applies a maximum of 6 stacks but you can move it around to be strategic as higher stacks in the same degree dont do anything)        

        so the idea of the fire element is pure compounding single target dps (like say bosses)

    Ice:
    - main attack (hailstorm) cd: 3.2s
        - choose a 3x3 area with your mouse. when you click it, a hailstorm will rain down for 3 seconds, dealing small damage every 0.25 seconds and slowing the enemy by 0.7x
    - special passive ability
        - ice skate: whenever the player moves, converts the previous tile into an ice tile, which lasts for 2 seconds. this ice tile slows any enemy by 0.7x for 2 seconds.
        - subzero: everytime an enemy is hit by a skill from the ice element (passive or active attack), every 0.2 seconds theres a 5% chance for the enemy to be *frozen* for a second. frozen enemies cannot move and enter the *brittle* state 
        - brittle: when an enemy is *frozen*, they receive 1.5x damage in the duration.
    - ultimate: blizzard of courage cd: 1m30s
        - enters a state where you take 60% less damage from all sources. in this state, your main attack cooldown is reduced to 1 seconds, and the chance for the freeze ability is increased to 70%.
            - in this state, everytime damage is taken, the cooldown of hailstorm is reduced by 0.3 seconds, allowing you to spam the chip damage more often. this passive has a cooldown of 0.2 seconds so its not broken when tanking

        so the idea of the ice element is tank and utility. this can be comboed with like fire or lightning, where you freeze as many enemies as possible with ice ult then change to fire or lightning to take advantage of brittle

ENGINEERING DEGREE (advanced purchaseable lightning weapon)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- scroll through 3 building modes, place towers using scraps
- tower cap: 6 total across all types, max 3 of any single type
- superconductored enemies grant towers +2 tiles detection range against them specifically

Tower types & scrap costs:
  Tesla Coil (8 scraps)
    - attacks all enemies in range, chains lightning between them
    - most versatile, highest cost
  Sniper Tower (6 scraps)
    - devastating single-target damage at very long range
    - does not alert nearby enemies (undetected shots)
  Machine Gun (5 scraps)
    - single target, highest sustained DPS of the three
    - cheapest since it rewards superconductor setup to shine

All towers can be destroyed. Superconductor range bonus applies to all three types.

Weapons

Sixteen weapons, four to an element. You carry one weapon for each element and switch with 1 to 4. Prices are in glorps at Glurp's shop. How the elements work together is in the guide.

Fire

Fire hits one thing hard and leaves Burn: up to 5 stacks, ticking every half second for 2, 4.5, 8, 13 or 20 damage a second plus 0.2% to 1% of the enemy's max HP a second (bosses take a quarter of that share). A Burn holds 4s after its last stack lands, then loses one a second. Fire also boils water into fog that stays dry for 15s. A 4-tile rush (6 during Scorch), about 0.7s to recover. Everything you rush through takes 12 and is staggered 0.3s. The tiles you crossed crack and, 0.6s later, erupt one after another: 16 to anything on or beside each one (once a dash per enemy), a moment of lava, and the water round them boiled off.

Did you know?
  • Lava never hurts by itself: standing in it adds a Burn stack every 0.8s.
  • A fire-immune enemy still takes Burn ticks: Burn is how fire hurts a fire elemental.
  • Burn ticks charge your fire gem even while you're holding another element.

Ice

Ice is the setup element. Every ice hit fills a Chill bar (a direct hit is about a fifth of it) that holds 5s and then drains; a full bar freezes the enemy into an ice cube for 3.5s that moves 1.8 times slower and is Brittle: 20% of its max HP is cut off at once (6% on bosses, never the last point). Damage from any other element while the cube holds locks that cut in for good and feeds the cube's shatter (10 plus 28% of what they dealt); whatever isn't locked in grows back a few seconds after it breaks. A 0.6s glide, 6 tiles long and 3 wide: the slowest dash, about 1.3s to recover. Everything in the strip gets a sixth of a Chill bar, each enemy at most once every 3s.

Did you know?
  • A shattered cube leaves a small plus-shaped puddle for 6s, and lightning loves wet enemies.
  • A cube can't be chilled again until it shatters, so more ice on it builds nothing.
  • Holding ice you move faster through water than on dry floor.

Second: a fan of shards that split; one that hits a marked enemy charges R. Press R at five: a gamble for 6s, Deadly Accuracy or Shard Storm.

Lightning

Lightning is weak on one enemy and strong on a crowd: it chains, forks and runs through water. Its status is Amped (usually 5s): a fire or ice hit on an Amped enemy is a reaction, and the Orb Emitter's orb only bounces onto Amped enemies. A Storm Fists or Orb Emitter hit on an enemy standing in water sends 60% of it through the whole connected pool (up to 30 tiles), hitting everything in it. An instant 3-tile blink, about 0.8s to recover. Where you land, a daze rolls out in rings over the 5 by 5 square round you, for 0.8s. Clear the Gauntlet to unlock it in the shop.

Did you know?
  • It's the only dash with no travel: you're simply there.
  • One pool can only be energised once every 0.4s, however fast you hit.
  • Resistance still counts in the water: a lightning-resistant enemy in an energised pool takes 40%.

Cosmic

Cosmic is the buff element: its weapons cast boons that keep working after you switch back to fire, ice or lightning. Its status is Hex, up to 3 stacks: +10%, +25% or +45% damage taken from everything and 40% slower steps, and a Hex is a reaction for all three other elements. Nothing resists cosmic, but every cosmic hit lands at 70% (50% on an elemental that's immune to the rest). Two hops 0.4s apart, each up to about 3.5 tiles to the open tile nearest your cursor, at any angle and through enemies (the second aims again, so you can bend the pair). You can't walk between them, and it recovers 1s after the second. Every tile crossed gets cosmic fluid for 1.8s, whose Hex lasts 3s. Clear the Void Rift to unlock it in the shop.

Did you know?
  • Holding cosmic, water doesn't slow you at all.
  • Cosmic weapons' own hits charge nothing: the cosmic gem fills from your other three elements' hits, at 1.25 times what they give their own gems.
  • The Adaptive Shade is the one enemy Hex slides right off.

Singularity

Cosmic Tier 3 250 glorps

Fires a seed of a black hole from the staff that speeds up toward your cursor, applying Hex (1s) to what it passes, and opens a well there. The well pulls enemies in and grows; the longer they stay, the more Hex stacks build (one every 2s, fading 1s after they leave). When it collapses it applies Dazed to everything inside. While it's out and 5s after: Event Horizon, -30% damage taken.

How it plays
Click (cooldown 12s) to fire a tiny black hole at the cursor, up to 9 tiles, through walls but never into a sealed room; it speeds up as it goes and Hexes what it passes for 1s. Where it lands a well opens for 5s, its reach growing from about 1.8 to 4.2 tiles: every 0.5s it deals 2 to everything inside, drags it a tile toward the middle and deepens its Hex (a stack per 2s inside, 3 at most, fading 1s after it leaves), and when it closes everything still inside is dazed 1s. While the well is open and for 5s after, you take 30% less damage (Event Horizon).
Levels
No Power levels. Wider Well, 3 levels (200 / 360 / 648 glorps): +0.5 tiles of radius a level. Long Horizon (200 / 360 / 648): the well lasts 1s longer a level. Event Shield (220 / 396 / 713): 35%, 40%, then 45% less damage taken.
Getting it

Tips

  • During Ascend, Event Horizon runs the whole time.
  • The seed is a dark heart with its own little accretion ring turning round it.

Ults

Each element has an ult, ready when its gem is full. E casts the one for the element you hold; Z X C V cast fire's, ice's, lightning's or cosmic's. Each grows in stages, bought at the shop with fragments and glorps. Every detail is in the guide.

Scorch the fire ult

  1. Stage IScorch, 8s, and you're locked in fire. You laugh, and meteors rain on the room: an opening volley of 4 to 7 (about one per 50 floor tiles), then 2 or 3 every 1.5 to 3s. Each hits everything within 2.2 to 3.2 tiles for 12 to 26 (bigger rocks, more), dazes it 1s, gives it a Super Burn stack, and leaves white-hot lava for 1.5s that Super Burns whoever stands in it. Once the volley is down your click is Meteor Strike, every 0.9s: 4 or 5 meteors land within about 3 tiles of it, each about 40 at its middle falling to 40% at its edge 1.8 tiles out, with a short daze and a Super Burn stack only on the enemy with the most HP it hit. A 6-tile aura gives one enemy a Super Burn stack every second (the healthiest likeliest), your dash goes 6 tiles and Super Burns what you rush through, you move about 1.8 times as fast, and every burn tick heals you 0.25% of your max HP (3% a second at most, 20% a cast, up to 20% past full). Super Burn: up to 3 stacks ticking five times a second, 30 a second plus 2.5% of max HP at 3, held 8s, then a stack off every 2.5s.
  2. Stage II5 fragments and 20 glorps: every meteor of the rain also lights a Supernova fuse on what it hits. 2s later it explodes for 15 and a Burn stack, and 70% of that (with a Burn stack) to everything within 2.5 tiles, lighting their fuses too; a neighbour's blast can relight each enemy twice at most. 5 fragments, 20 glorps
  3. Stage IIINot out yet.
Charging
Every fire hit fills the fire gem, Burn and Super Burn ticks included, whichever element you're holding; each weapon has its own rate. A hit on an enemy carrying another element's status counts 3 times, and one on an enemy another element hit in the last 1.5s counts 1.75 times. It can't charge for 8.5s after you cast, and charge past a full gem is banked as up to +50% damage on your next Scorch.
Did you know?
  • Its healing can take you up to 120% of your max HP.
  • Each meteor sits half-buried in its crater, cooling from white-hot to dark over 3s.
  • Only the fire ult adds Super Burn stacks, but any ordinary Burn landing on one keeps it alight.

Blizzard the ice ult

  1. Stage IBlizzard, 20s, and you can switch elements all through it. It opens with a wave out to 18 tiles that puts everything it reaches in Deep Freeze: fire, lightning and cosmic do 2 times damage to it and ice 1.2, and 3s in it all shatters by itself for 100 plus 15% of each enemy's current HP (bosses take the 100), plus 30% of what the other elements dealt it. For the rest of it a freezing ring 7 tiles round you cubes an enemy at a third of a Chill bar, and those cubes shatter after 1.2s for 25 plus 3% of current HP. Gale Storm: every 5s a tornado gathers round you, is flung at the crosshair and hunts enemies for 7s, chipping 1 and chilling everything in it twice a second, and grows from 1.5 to 4 tiles round as it holds them (the next one forms at the biggest yet). The price: you take 1.5 times damage the whole time, start slowed (steps 1.9 times as long, easing off over 5s), and at the end you're encased in ice for 3s, untouchable but unable to move, act or dash. Press X again (after its first second) to end it early, straight into the ice.
  2. Stage II5 fragments and 20 glorps: the Deep Freeze shatter hits half again as hard, 150 plus 22.5% of current HP (bosses take 150). 5 fragments, 20 glorps
  3. Stage IIINot out yet.
Charging
Ice hits fill the ice gem, and making a cube and shattering one add a little more. Reactions count 3 times and residue 1.75 times; the Fractal Shard's Shard Storm doubles it. It can't charge for the whole Blizzard, its 3s of ice included (ending it early ends that too), and charge past a full gem is banked as up to +50% damage on the next one.

  3. Stage III15 fragments and 80 glorps: three layers. The Voltslime

Where
The Gauntlet, in its boss arena on the storm tower's roof (the last floor). Tower floor 10, then every 40 floors (50, 90, ...), in a 32x24 arena. The Training Ground can summon it.
Health
1,200 (8x a slime), the same whatever the level. In the Tower its story HP is multiplied by the floor's enemy HP scale / 2.2, which is x1.08 a floor: 2,399 on floor 10, 52,113 on floor 50.
What it does
It slams before every attack: 0.7s of wind-up, then 12 damage on the lit tiles within 2 steps of it (no diagonals). Then it runs five attacks in order, round and round: Scatter (4 rings of 10 bolts, 0.8s apart, each ring filling the last one's gaps), Homing (3 slow purple orbs that chase you for 7s, 20 each), Spawner (4 sparking 30 HP slimes that zap 5 into you every 0.6s if you stand next to one), Spiral (48 bolts wound out in 4 turns over about 4s) and Ricochet (16 bolts that bounce off walls up to twice). Where

Health
3,000 (20x a slime), the same whatever the level. In the Tower its story HP is multiplied by the floor's enemy HP scale / 2.2 (x1.08 a floor): 12,947 on floor 20, 281,270 on floor 60.

Glossary

The game's own words, A to Z, as the shop and the guide use them. Words in this colour anywhere on this page jump here.

Amped
Lightning's mark on an enemy: a hit from fire, ice or cosmic on it is a reaction, the Orb Emitter's orb bounces only between Amped enemies, and some books and relics hit Amped enemies harder.
Bleed
On you: every tile you move while it lasts hurts (a dash, every tile it covers). Stand still or pick your steps. From a Hunter's slashes, a Torpedo Diver and a bursting water bubble.
Blue Burn
A burn stack from Blue Flame. It does not tick: when the burn's hold runs out it spreads, turning the ordinary stacks blue one by one, then each blue stack bursts for 25 + 2% of max HP through resistance (bosses take a quarter of the share). Shorter than Burn, far more damage in all.
Blue Flame (R)
Ignition's ability: when it's ready (16s after the last), press R and for 5s the flame burns blue-hot. It ticks half again as fast (kindle fills in 3s), ignores resistance, and now and then lays a Blue Burn stack.
Brittle
While an enemy is an ice cube, 20% of its max HP is cut off (6% on a boss). When the cube ends that health slowly grows back; damage from fire, lightning and cosmic while it holds locks part of it in for good.
Burn
Fire damage over time, up to 5 stacks: a little flat damage plus a share of the enemy's max HP every half second (5 stacks: 20 a second plus 1% of max HP; bosses take a quarter of the share). Stacks from different hits add up, and fire weapons feed it.
Capacitor (G)
A Circuit Breaker clamp (orange): the enemy takes no current; it stores it instead, and every 3s bursts, forking the stored charge out to every enemy within 2.5 tiles and sharing it between them (a little extra for each one it reaches). Cut its wire (hold G at it) to burst it at once. A capacitor is never Amped: it is all damage, no reactions.
Cheerleader Slime
Rushes to her friends, then performs on a pop-up stage for six seconds, taking half damage: everything near her is encouraged.
Chevrons
Little arrows over anything moving faster (up) or slower (down) than usual, you included.
Chill
Chill builds with every ice hit; a full bar freezes the enemy into an ice cube, applying Brittle. Chill alone doesn't slow it: the cube does.
Circuit
With the Circuit Breaker: wired enemies that arc to each other chain into a ring through you, out along your first wire, through each enemy in turn and home along the last (the wires to the ones in between go thin: the current runs through the arcs). A ring of two does double, and each enemy past that adds half again (three x2.5, four x3). Its live and insulated clamps are Amped while it stays closed.
Constellation
Hold V past halfway: you drop into a rift, then place an anchor and click where to strike: 4 stars (a pair and its mirror) crash together there, hitting everything in the way. Two voidlets come through with you and fight at your side.
Constellation+
Hold V to the end: untouchable until the slam, healing all the while, and the slam applies Dazed. Each throw is 8 stars crashing in through walls, applying Hex to what they hit, and 5 voidlets come through: 3 warriors and 2 voidbeams.
Cosmic fluid
Cosmic's ground: left by the Void Blade, the cosmic dash and Ascend. Enemies standing in it gain Hex every 2s. How long it lasts depends on where it came from (a fully charged Void Blade's lasts longest).
Current (R)
Press R: a big blob of current runs down the wire nearest your aim, on through the arcs, and back to you along the last wire. Every enemy it passes is dazed, takes a small hit, and is Amped for a few seconds (capacitors aside).
Dazed
Stunned for a moment: no moving, no attacking. Deals no damage by itself.
Deadly Accuracy (R)
One side of the Fractal Shard's gamble: for 6s five shards circle you. Click and they line up half a tile apart, converge on that point and fly on past it, half again as hard, and every hit a crit.
Deep Freeze
The ice ult's freeze: held still until the ult shatters it. Fire, lightning and cosmic do x2 to it, ice x1.2; the shatter adds back part of what the other elements dealt.
Discouraged
Near a Cheerleader Slime when she died: for five seconds it moves at 60% speed but takes only 60% damage (the grey pall and broken hearts).
Dripping
On you, for 8s: every tile you walk becomes water, and it stays until fire dries it. Dangerous with water enemies about.
Drowner
Sinks to attack. If you're standing in water when it commits, it lands instantly: there's no dodge. Fight it on dry ground.
Encouraged
Cheered on by a Cheerleader Slime: for six seconds it moves faster and hits three times as hard (the pink pulse and hearts), unless she dies first. The extra damage shows in pink beside the hit.
Fog
Water your fire dried. It takes no new water until it clears.
Frost Shield (R)
5s of 35% less damage taken, and it applies Chill to whoever hits you.
Hex
Cosmic's mark on an enemy, its stacks orbiting it (up to 3). Takes more damage from everything: +10% at one stack, +25% at two, +45% at three, and slowed. Fire, ice or lightning hitting a hexed enemy is a reaction (ult charge x3). Ascend's supernova hits harder for each stack. How long it lasts depends on where it came from: the Void Blade's lasts longest.
Ice cube
A full chill bar freezes an enemy into a cube: it moves at about half speed, and it shatters by itself after a few seconds. While it holds it is Brittle.
Ignition (R)
Ignition's ability, now Blue Flame: press R for 5s of blue-hot flame that ticks faster and ignores resistance.
Insulator (G)
A Circuit Breaker clamp (blue): its enemy takes only 30% of the current, and the whole web (lines, clamps, bursts and currents) does 35% less for each insulator on it, but every daze the web gives lasts 60% longer, and its enemy stays Amped longest. Control for damage.
Kindle
Ignition's mark, on the first two enemies its flame finds. The two stay marked till you detonate them (or they cool out): a third in the flame isn't marked. It fills while the flame is on them (about 4.5s) and holds while you keep it there; left alone it cools and fizzles. Full, it is overkindled: the flame does real damage to it (plus 0.2% of its max HP a tick), it catches Burn half the time, and a second ring fills, up to 40% of a bar. 3s after the flame leaves it goes off by itself. Press G (Detonate) to set every mark off at any time: the fuller, the far bigger the blast (a full bar also takes 6% of its max HP). It stays marked and starts again from whatever the second ring stored.
Lava
A molten patch on the floor. It deals no damage itself: it applies a Burn stack to anything standing in it every 0.8s.
Lava tile
A molten tile your fire leaves behind. Enemies standing on it take a Burn stack every 0.8s.
Live Wire
The Circuit Breaker's plain clamp (yellow): current runs through the enemy and hurts it every 0.3s. Amped while its circuit is closed.
Magma Surge (R)
Dump the staff's stored heat: everything round you is thrown back and gets Burn, and a wave of lava rolls outward. More heat, a bigger surge. Spending it drops the staff back to its first tier.
Mark
An astral mark, a gold ring left by the Astrolabe's stars. Hitting the enemy pops it for ult charge.
Momentum
Stacks from punching and dashing: +7% damage and +8% speed each. Fades if you stop.
Poison
Damage over time on you, and any healing you get is cut by 60% while it lasts. Only one dose at a time: a new one restarts the clock, it never stacks.
Poison pool
Left by Rooters and Budding Slimes. Standing in it doses you with poison.
Reaction
A hit on an enemy carrying another element's status (burning, chilled or cubed, amped, hexed). It charges your ult three times as much as a plain hit.
Residue
A hit on an enemy another element hit in the last 1.5s, with no status of it left: ult charge x1.75.
Resistance
A ring under an enemy in an element's colour: that element's hits land at 40%. A ring split in two or three resists two or three elements. Nothing resists cosmic.
Rift drop
You pick a spot within 14 tiles and slam down onto it. Enemies under you take a hit and are slowed.
Rooted
On you: stuck where you stand for a moment. You can still attack.
Shard Storm (R)
The other side of the Fractal Shard's gamble, the ult charger: it heals 10% of your max HP when it lands, and for 6s ice ult charge is doubled and every shot is a wide fan of seven chip shards, about three a second.
Stunned
Can't move or attack until it wears off.
Super Burn
Burn's bigger form, from the fire ult: 3 stacks at most, ticking five times a second for a little flat damage plus a share of max HP (3 stacks: 30 a second plus 2.5% of max HP; bosses take a quarter of the share). It lasts far longer than Burn (8s, then a stack every 2.5s), and any Burn landing on it keeps it going, though only the fire ult adds stacks. It melts things rather than thudding them.
Supernova
A blast at the end of Ascend: more damage for every Hex stack on each enemy in reach.
Tidecaller
Doesn't fight: every enemy near it attacks faster, a little on dry floor and far faster standing in water.
Twin Collapse
Click to place the anchor (right-click moves it). Every click after throws: the anchored star and its twin rush together and implode where you clicked, pulling enemies in and applying Hex to what they pass. The farther from the anchor, the harder and wider.
Voidlets
Little void allies that draw enemy attention. Warriors chase and punch. Voidbeams lock on, then fire a wide beam that pierces and ricochets off walls. They stay with you until they fall.
Wards
Orbiting shields. Each one eats one projectile, then shoves nearby enemies back.
Water
Supercharges the water enemies standing in it: Wave Riders charge sooner, Ripplerunners race, a Tidal Guard's hit pins you, a Tidecaller makes everything in it attack far faster (a little faster on dry floor). Fire dries it to fog.

Weapons

Twenty weapons, five to an element. You carry one weapon for each element and switch with 1 to 4. Prices are in glorps at Glurp's shop. How the elements work together is in the guide.

Fire

Fire hits one thing hard and leaves Burn: up to 5 stacks, ticking every half second for 2, 4.5, 8, 13 or 20 damage a second plus 0.2% to 1% of the enemy's max HP a second (bosses take a quarter of that share). A Burn holds 4s after its last stack lands, then loses one a second. Fire also boils water into fog that stays dry for 15s. A 4-tile rush (6 during Scorch), about 0.7s to recover. Everything you rush through takes 12 and is staggered 0.3s. The tiles you crossed crack and, 0.6s later, erupt one after another: 16 to anything on or beside each one (once a dash per enemy), a moment of lava, and the water round them boiled off.

Did you know?
  • Lava never hurts by itself: standing in it adds a Burn stack every 0.8s.
  • A fire-immune enemy still takes Burn ticks: Burn is how fire hurts a fire elemental.
  • Burn ticks charge your fire gem even while you're holding another element.

Ice

Ice is the setup element. Every ice hit fills a Chill bar (a direct hit is about a fifth of it) that holds 5s and then drains; a full bar freezes the enemy into an ice cube for 3.5s that moves 1.8 times slower and is Brittle: 20% of its max HP is cut off at once (6% on bosses, never the last point). Damage from any other element while the cube holds locks that cut in for good and feeds the cube's shatter (10 plus 28% of what they dealt); whatever isn't locked in grows back a few seconds after it breaks. A 0.6s glide, 6 tiles long and 3 wide: the slowest dash, about 1.3s to recover. Everything in the strip gets a sixth of a Chill bar, each enemy at most once every 3s.

Did you know?
  • A shattered cube leaves a small plus-shaped puddle for 6s, and lightning loves wet enemies.
  • A cube can't be chilled again until it shatters, so more ice on it builds nothing.
  • Holding ice you move faster through water than on dry floor.

Lightning

Lightning is weak on one enemy and strong on a crowd: it chains, forks and runs through water. Its status is Amped (usually 5s): a fire or ice hit on an Amped enemy is a reaction, and the Orb Emitter's orb only bounces onto Amped enemies. A Storm Fists or Orb Emitter hit on an enemy standing in water sends 60% of it through the whole connected pool (up to 30 tiles), hitting everything in it. An instant 3-tile blink, about 0.8s to recover. Where you land, a daze rolls out in rings over the 5 by 5 square round you, for 0.8s. Clear the Gauntlet to unlock it in the shop.

Did you know?
  • It's the only dash with no travel: you're simply there.
  • One pool can only be energised once every 0.4s, however fast you hit.
  • Resistance still counts in the water: a lightning-resistant enemy in an energised pool takes 40%.

1,200 (8x a slime), the same whatever the level. In the Tower its story HP is multiplied by the floor's enemy HP scale / 2.2, which is x1.08 a floor: 2,399 on floor 10, 52,113 on floor 50.
What it does
It slams before every attack: 0.7s of wind-up, then 12 damage on the lit tiles within 2 steps of it (no diagonals). Then it runs five attacks in order, round and round: Scatter (4 rings of 10 bolts, 0.8s apart, each ring filling the last one's gaps), Homing (3 slow purple orbs that chase you for 7s, 20 each), Spawner (4 sparking 30 HP slimes that zap 5 into you every 0.6s if you stand next to one), Spiral (48 bolts wound out in 4 turns over about 4s) and Ricochet (16 bolts that bounce off walls up to twice). Every bolt does 10. It has no health phases: the loop at 5% is the loop at 100%.
Beating it
It's hit anywhere in the 3x3 tiles round it and resists nothing. Keep out of the slam's 2 steps, step in to hit between attacks, and walk the homing orbs into a wall (they burst on one). Ice fills a chill bar over it at half the usual rate; when it's full it's a brittle cube for 2.5s that chips off 6% of its max HP (it grows back a few seconds later unless you hit the cube with another element), then breaks for 10 plus 28% of what the other elements dealt it, and it shrugs off chill for 3s. The ice ult, and ice's piercing spikes and ice blocks, freeze it solid for 2.2s: it can't start a new attack and your next hit does 2.5x. Lightning chains jump onto it from its sparking slimes, and cosmic hits Hex it.
Rewards
Story: 80 glorps every time it dies, and 2 fragments the first time (1 every time after). Then the Gauntlet's gold chest: lightning unlocked, and on your first clear 3 more fragments and 300 glorps. Tower: the 80 glorps still drop, plus a boss floor's rewards: 5x a floor's pay (139 glorps on floor 10, before boosts), 65 dust on floor 10, 3 sealed books, a garden seed, a relic you don't own yet and a boon pick (from floor 50, also a 35% chance of a sapling). Clearing floor 10 earns First Steps (200 glorps), and it counts toward Kingslayer.

  • In the Gauntlet it's fast asleep when you walk in: grey, its crown lying on the floor beside it, up to six little slimes dozing round it. Your slime lobs three rocks at its head (bonk, bonk, BONK!!) until it wakes in a fury, its crown hops back on and its brood jumps up scared.

The Void Sovereign

Where
The Void Rift: its boss arena, behind a door that takes the level's 3 keys. Tower floor 20, then every 40 floors (60, 100, ...), in a 32x24 arena. The Training Ground can summon it.
Health
3,000 (20x a slime), the same whatever the level. In the Tower its story HP is multiplied by the floor's enemy HP scale / 2.2 (x1.08 a floor): 12,947 on floor 20, 281,270 on floor 60.
What it does
It picks attacks at random, never the same one twice running, resting 1.8s between them (1.3s from 60% health, 0.9s from 30%). From the start: Void Mines (three waves dropped round you, 6 a wave, 8 from 60%, 10 from 30%; each blows after 1.2s for 10 and roots you 3s), Singularity Slimes (8, then 13, then 18 slow 50 HP slimes that stay), and the Laser Grid (three grids of beams in a row, each flickering 1.4s first, 14 if you're on a line). At 60% it roars, smashes the four stone pillars in its arena and adds Dark Matter Fragments (it vanishes into 4 orbiting crystals that throw rings of ripples, 12 each and 50% harder every 5s, until all four are broken) and the Stomp (it leaps, a circle follows you for 2.5s, flashes for 0.8s, and it lands for 40 and a 2s root within 2.5 tiles). At 30% it adds the Void Maw (4 hops through portals, each landing beside you and firing 16 bouncing pellets all round, 11 each), the Event Horizon (7s of reflecting your hits while a pulse every 1.5s throws you back for 12) and the Singularity Collapse (9s of dark pulling you in faster and faster; if it can see you at the end you're dragged to the middle at 1 HP and rooted 2s).
Beating it
Its body takes hits within about 2 tiles of its middle and resists nothing, but not while it's gone (the fragments, the stomp's leap, the maw's hops), during the Collapse, or in the Event Horizon. Keep moving for the mines and the stomp, and stand in the gaps of the laser grid. Break the four fragments fast: when the last one goes it comes back taking double damage for 4s. In the Event Horizon stand inside its ring or more than 6 tiles out; in the Collapse put a wall between you and the middle of the room (the cover is outlined on the floor), or be inside the ice ult's shell. The ice ult freezes it solid for 2.2s and your first hit after does 2x; ice's chill on it builds at half the usual rate into the same brittle cube as the Voltslime's.
Rewards
Story: 2 fragments the first time it dies (1 every time after), then the Void Rift's gold chest: cosmic unlocked, and on your first clear 4 more fragments and 400 glorps. Tower: 8 scraps where it falls, plus a boss floor's rewards: 5x a floor's pay (431 glorps on floor 20, before boosts), 70 dust on floor 20, 3 sealed books, a garden seed, a 35% chance of a sapling for the orchard, a relic you don't own yet and a boon pick. It counts toward Kingslayer (5 Tower bosses).
Did you know?
  • Its Singularity Collapse drags every other enemy on the level into the middle and kills them, its own Singularity slimes included.
  • At 60% health it roars and smashes the big stone pillars at the corners of its arena to rubble.
  • It never walks: it only leaves its spot to leap on you or to hop through its Void Maw.

Where

The Core

Where
Only in the Training Ground, on the TRIAL tab's Fracture Rhythm Run.
Health
1,500 (10x a slime). A run longer than 55s gives it more, in step with the run's length. Your notes each take a share of its health, so its size never changes how many notes it takes.
What it does
The floor is gone. You step onto slabs that form one at a time on the beat, in four phases (one per row) at 66, 82, 96 and 110 BPM (times the SPEED setting), and each phase's rhythms are busier. It floats out of reach and never moves: your playing is what hurts it. Each PERFECT flies into it for a set share of its health, a GOOD for 55% of that, a BAD for nothing. 8, 16 or 32 PERFECTs in a row (GROOVE) multiply that by 1.25, 1.5 or 2, and your weapon hits too. Rewards
None: a CLEARED! banner and your best combo.

Glossary

The game's own words, A to Z, as the shop and the guide use them. Words in this colour anywhere on this page jump here.

Amped
Lightning's mark on an enemy: a hit from fire, ice or cosmic on it is a reaction, the Orb Emitter's orb bounces only between Amped enemies, and some books and relics hit Amped enemies harder.
Big Crunch
The transformations' slam: every enemy with Hex on it within 6 tiles (8 in the full form) is pulled in, and each of its Hex stacks is cashed in as damage (12 plus 6% of its max HP a stack; bosses less), the stacks gone.
Bleed
On you: every tile you move while it lasts hurts (a dash, every tile it covers). Stand still or pick your steps. From a Hunter's slashes and a bursting water bubble.
Blue Burn
A burn stack from Blue Flame. It does not tick: when the burn's hold runs out it spreads, turning the ordinary stacks blue one by one, then each blue stack bursts for 14 + 1.2% of max HP through resistance (bosses take a quarter of the share). Shorter than Burn, far more damage in all.
Blue Flame (R)
Ignition's ability: when it's ready (16s after the last), press R and for 5s the flame burns blue-hot. It ticks half again as fast (kindle fills in 3s), ignores resistance, and now and then lays a Blue Burn stack.
Boom
The end of a Thunder Glaive streak (its second runs out, no one is left to pivot off, or 10 links): a fuse races down the ropes in the order you laid them and they all go off at once, 4 to everything within a tile of one and 5 more for every link (9 for one link, 54 for ten; half again if it's Amped), and a half-second daze; insulated enemies take nothing, and a rope over water runs through the pool. It powers the network: two volts back a link, and from two links an overload. Then it leaves a cage. With coils up, a streak of two links or more brings a bolt from the sky down on each tesla coil, a light hit round it.
Boreal Aura
While you hold the Boreal Trident, a haze of frost hangs round you (the ice ult's, fainter), 3.5 tiles out: enemy shots in it fly at about a third of their speed, frosting over as they go. Bullets, globs, slugs, shells and boss shots alike.
Brittle
While an enemy is an ice cube, 20% of its max HP is cut off (6% on a boss). When the cube ends that health slowly grows back; damage from fire, lightning and cosmic while it holds locks part of it in for good.
Burn
Fire damage over time, up to 5 stacks: a little flat damage plus a share of the enemy's max HP every half second (5 stacks: 20 a second plus 1% of max HP; bosses take a quarter of the share). Stacks from different hits add up, and fire weapons feed it.
Cage
What a Thunder Glaive streak's boom leaves, for 6s: a post where each enemy you pivoted off stood, live fences post to post (closed round from three posts), and a fence from each post to the nearest tesla coil within 8 tiles (while you have volts). It's part of the network: every Amped enemy inside it takes the current. An enemy that crosses a fence, or steps onto or off one, is shocked: a small hit, Amped and dazed for a moment (once a second a fence). Insulated enemies shrug it off. A new streak's cage takes the old one's place.
Capacitor (G)
A Circuit Breaker clamp (orange): the enemy takes no current; it stores it instead, and every 3s bursts, forking the stored charge out to every enemy within 2.5 tiles and sharing it between them (a little extra for each one it reaches). Cut its wire (hold G at it) to burst it at once. A capacitor is never Amped: it is all damage, no reactions.
Cheerleader Slime
Rushes to her friends, then performs on a pop-up stage for six seconds, taking half damage: everything near her is encouraged.
Chevrons
Little arrows over anything moving faster (up) or slower (down) than usual, you included.
Chill
Chill builds with every ice hit; a full bar freezes the enemy into an ice cube, applying Brittle. Chill alone doesn't slow it: the cube does.
Circuit
With the Circuit Breaker: wired enemies that arc to each other chain into a ring through you, out along your first wire, through each enemy in turn and home along the last (the wires to the ones in between go thin: the current runs through the arcs). A ring of two does double, and each enemy past that adds half again (three x2.5, four x3). Its live and insulated clamps are Amped while it stays closed.
Constellation
Hold V past halfway: you drop into a rift, then place an anchor and click where to strike: 4 stars (a pair and its mirror) crash together there, hitting everything in the way. Two voidlets come through with you and fight at your side.
Constellation+
Hold V to the end: untouchable until the slam, healing all the while, and the slam applies Dazed. Each throw is 8 stars crashing in through walls, applying Hex to what they hit, and 5 voidlets come through: 3 warriors and 2 voidbeams.
Cosmic fluid
Cosmic's ground: left by the Void Blade, the cosmic dash and Ascend. Enemies standing in it gain Hex every 2s. How long it lasts depends on where it came from (a fully charged Void Blade's lasts longest).
Current (R)
Press R: a big blob of current runs down the wire nearest your aim, on through the arcs, and back to you along the last wire. Every enemy it passes is dazed, takes a small hit, and is Amped for a few seconds (capacitors aside).
Dazed
Stunned for a moment: no moving, no attacking. Deals no damage by itself.
Deep Freeze
The ice ult's freeze: held still until the ult shatters it. Fire, lightning and cosmic do x2 to it, ice x1.2; the shatter adds back part of what the other elements dealt.
Discouraged
Near a Cheerleader Slime when she died: for five seconds it moves at 60% speed but takes only 60% damage (the grey pall and broken hearts).
Dripping
On you, for 8s (4s from a Torpedo Diver's shot): every tile you step off becomes water (never the one you stand on), and it stays until fire dries it. Dangerous with water enemies about.
Drowner
Sinks to attack. If you're standing in water when it commits, it lands instantly: there's no dodge. Fight it on dry ground.
Ember
A tile the Ember Scythe sets alight, burning for 7s. Every second, each enemy on it or beside it has a 30% chance of a Super Burn stack (Kindling raises it).
Ember Soul
Shaken out of every enemy an Ember Scythe swing cuts. Walk near one and it flies to you (you carry up to 30); 5 make a Heat Wave.
Encouraged
Cheered on by a Cheerleader Slime: for six seconds it moves faster and hits three times as hard (the pink pulse and hearts), unless she dies first. The extra damage shows in pink beside the hit.
Fog
Water your fire dried. It takes no new water until it clears.
Fractal Bloom (R)
The Fractal Shard's R, once five of its shots have broken all the way down (or hit an ice cube): a seed crystal flies to where you aim and blooms, a snowflake of crystal bursting out of the floor (six arms, each forking twice), cracking and chilling what it grows under, its outermost tips freezing it into a cube. A moment later the whole flake shatters for a second hit.
Frost Rush
The moment a Boreal Trident hurl lands having caught something: for 4s (and 3s after it's back in your hand, if that's later) its combo runs nearly twice as fast: the thrust, the swipe and the leap, and the window to keep it going with them. A chime, the words and a flash start it; you glow frost-bright the whole time and a white sliver over the combo pips drains; it fizzles off when it ends. Clump them with the throw, then dump the combo into the heap.
Frost Shield (R)
5s of 35% less damage taken, and it applies Chill to whoever hits you.
Frostbite
The Boreal Trident's status: 3s a hit (6s at most), shown as a violet snowflake by the frost bar. While it lasts the enemy's chill grows on its own, nearly half a stack a second, until it freezes into a cube. Only two things give it: the leap's slam and the Squall frostkin's gust (the rime the trident leaves on the floor is only its look).
Frostkin (R)
The Boreal Trident's R, for 48 frost (96 banked at most), and with it a frost shell round you worth 20% of your max HP, taking hits before your health with no time limit till it breaks (a call while it's up tops it back up, never above): every enemy a hit of the combo strikes is frost (the thrust and the swipe 1, the slam 2; 5 enemies a hit at most); throws and cubes give none. Three ice wisps, one of each kind (melee, hail, Squall), fighting beside you till they're killed (9 at most: past that the oldest shatter away). Their HP is a share of your max HP (a quarter for the melee one, less for the others), they get half your defence and half your damage bonus; each shows its HP. The melee one pulls the enemies within 3 tiles of it onto itself and nips them from a tile of its own, chilling what's within 2 tiles. The hail one keeps 3 to 5 tiles off with a clear line and every 1.6s calls a hail of ice spears onto the 3x3 tiles round its target, a light hit and a little chill on each, the middle one last and hardest. The Squall keeps 3 to 6 tiles off and every 4.5s breathes in and blows a gust 4 tiles wide and nearly 10 long through everything: a small hit and frostbite on all of it, no push. Frostkin Pack adds a hail one, then a Squall.
Heat Wave (R)
Five ember souls: the scythe points at the nearest enemy (the cursor only with no one about) and a narrow crescent of heat, 3 tiles across, pulses 9 tiles out, two crescents of fire behind it. Each crescent gives everything it reaches a Super Burn stack; the ground they cross flares white-hot for 2s, and three embers are left burning along it for 12s, keeping it going. Casting it heals you 4% of your max HP.
Hex
Cosmic's mark on an enemy, its stacks orbiting it (up to 3). Takes more damage from everything: +10% at one stack, +25% at two, +45% at three, and slowed. Fire, ice or lightning hitting a hexed enemy is a reaction (ult charge x3). Ascend's supernova hits harder for each stack. How long it lasts depends on where it came from: the Void Blade's lasts longest.
Hurl
Hold the click with the Boreal Trident, then let go: it's thrown straight down your aim, skimming low, 5 tiles, up to 8 drawn a second (its line shows while you hold it), once every 7s (Swift Recall: 0.6s less a level; the thin bar under the frost bar fills, a ping when it's ready). What it hits is impaled on its tines, and the rip current behind it drags in everything within two and a half tiles of its line, never through a wall (a light hit); rime crystallises over the floor it passes and melts in a moment (only its look). At the first wall (or boss) it pins them all: a harder hit and a long daze, each sealed in rime; at its range it drops them where the sea crashes in, a light hit and a short daze, and the wake behind it flash-freezes back down its path. Then it flies back to your hand; till it's back you can't stab. A throw that caught something brings frost rush. With riptide, click again while it flies to ride its wake.
Ice cube
A full chill bar freezes an enemy into a cube: it moves at about half speed, and it shatters by itself after a few seconds. While it holds it is Brittle.
Ignition (R)
Ignition's ability, now Blue Flame: press R for 5s of blue-hot flame that ticks faster and ignores resistance.
Insulator (G)
A Circuit Breaker clamp (blue): its enemy takes only 30% of the current, and the whole web (lines, clamps, bursts and currents) does 35% less for each insulator on it, but every daze the web gives lasts 60% longer, and its enemy stays Amped longest. Control for damage.
Kindle
Ignition's mark, on the first two enemies its flame finds. The two stay marked till you detonate them (or they cool out): a third in the flame isn't marked. It fills while the flame is on them (about 4.5s) and holds while you keep it there; left alone it cools and fizzles. Full, it is overkindled: the flame does real damage to it (plus 0.2% of its max HP a tick), it catches Burn half the time, and a second ring fills, up to 40% of a bar. 3s after the flame leaves it goes off by itself. Press G (Detonate) to set every mark off at any time: the fuller, the far bigger the blast (a full bar also takes 6% of its max HP). It stays marked and starts again from whatever the second ring stored.
Lava
A molten patch on the floor. It deals no damage itself: it applies a Burn stack to anything standing in it every 0.8s.
Lava tile
A molten tile your fire leaves behind. Enemies standing on it take a Burn stack every 0.8s.
Live Wire
The Circuit Breaker's plain clamp (yellow): current runs through the enemy and hurts it every 0.3s. Amped while its circuit is closed.
Magma Surge (R)
Dump the staff's stored heat: everything round you is thrown back and gets Burn, and a wave of lava rolls outward. More heat, a bigger surge. Spending it drops the staff back to its first tier.
Mark
An astral mark, a gold ring left by the Astrolabe's stars. Hitting the enemy pops it for ult charge.
Momentum
Stacks from punching and dashing: +7% damage and +8% speed each. Fades if you stop.
Overload
What a Thunder Glaive boom of two links or more sets off: 0.6s a link past the first (5.4s at 10 links). The network's current runs twice as often and twice as hard, for no more volts a second.
Pivot Strike
The Thunder Glaive's click: the enemy nearest the cursor within 5 tiles is your pivot (an arc reaches for it from the blade). You dash through it and on past it: 4 tiles past it from close up, down to 1.5 from far off, stopping short of walls and bosses. Untouchable as you go. Everything on the line takes a hit and is Amped, unless it's insulated. Click another pivot within a second of landing to go on: each link hits 20% harder, never off the same enemy twice running, up to 10 links. With no one else in reach, a boss: you dash at it, stop short and strike it.
Poison
Damage over time on you, and any healing you get is cut by 60% while it lasts. Only one dose at a time: a new one restarts the clock, it never stacks.
Poison pool
Left by Rooters and Budding Slimes. Standing in it doses you with poison.
Reaction
A hit on an enemy carrying another element's status (burning, chilled or cubed, amped, hexed). It charges your ult three times as much as a plain hit.
Residue
A hit on an enemy another element hit in the last 1.5s, with no status of it left: ult charge x1.75.
Resistance
A ring under an enemy in an element's colour: that element's hits land at 40%. A ring split in two or three resists two or three elements. Nothing resists cosmic.
Rewind (R)
The Pendulum's R, on the cooldown it shares with its Time Lock (30s after a Rewind, 12s after a Time Lock): you're pulled back along your own path to where you were 3s ago. The HP you've lost since comes back (a heal since stays) and every ailment you picked up since goes. Ults, gadgets and stamina you spent stay spent. It never takes you back through a door, a lift or a fall: only as far as you've been in this room.
Rift drop
You pick a spot within 14 tiles and slam down onto it: a big crunch. Enemies under you take a hit and are slowed.
Rime
The Rimeguard's charge, while it's your ice weapon, whatever element you're on: every hit an enemy lands on you is one (a big one, 15% of your max HP or more, two; one more on the raised shield; a quarter of a second apart at most), frost growing on you as it fills. Burns and bleeds don't count. At 8 it goes off: Shatterback. Go 5s without a hit and a charge melts every 1.5s.
Rime Marks
The Boreal Trident's marks, crystals over an enemy (3 at most, for 4s after the last): the thrust leaves 2 on what it hits, the swipe 1 on everything in its arc. The leap's slam shatters them when its rime erupts: 40% more of the eruption's damage a mark, a second more frostbite a mark, and a frost toward frostkin a mark. The more shattered at once, the bigger the burst.
Riptide
The Boreal Trident's last upgrade, sold once Long Hurl is maxed: click again while the hurl flies and you ride its wake, surfing along behind it on the rip current, and you're set down on free floor a tile short of where it stops (it waits there, stuck, till you arrive); its frost rush starts as you land (a boss stopping it sets you down beside it). You can be hit all the way, there are no dashes, ults, gadgets or swaps till you're down, and the throw's 7s only starts when you land. You stop at the edge of the room you set off in, and never ride through a sealed door.
Rooted
On you: stuck where you stand for a moment. You can still attack.
Rope
The crackling line of current each pivot strike leaves along the way you dashed. It's part of the Thunder Glaive's network as soon as it's laid, and stays live 6s after its streak's boom (16 at most): twice a second, while you have volts, every Amped enemy within a tile of one takes the current, and it jumps on from there to Amped enemies up to 2.5 tiles apart (12 at most). Each takes 9, 10% more for every other enemy in the chain, at most half again. Insulated enemies don't conduct. A boss in reach takes 40% of a hop and passes nothing on.
Shatterback
The Rimeguard's burst: on its own when rime reaches 8, on any element, or early with R while you hold it (3 or more). Ice spikes burst out of the floor round you (2.5 tiles, 3.5 at full): everything in it takes 15 plus 40% of your max HP (by how full it was) and half a bar of Chill, so something already chilled freezes into a cube, ready for whatever you're holding.
Stunned
Can't move or attack until it wears off.
Super Burn
Burn's bigger form: 3 stacks at most, ticking five times a second for a little flat damage plus a share of max HP (3 stacks: 30 a second plus 2.5% of max HP; bosses take a quarter of the share). It lasts far longer than Burn (8s, then a stack every 2.5s). It takes over from Burn: landing, it puts out any Burn stacks, and while it lasts the enemy can't Burn (Burn landing on it only keeps it going). From the fire ult and the Ember Scythe. It melts things rather than thudding them.
Supernova
A blast at the end of Ascend: more damage for every Hex stack on each enemy in reach.
Taunted
Struck by the Rimeguard's free swing (not the jab behind its shield): for 3s it comes for you, forgetting decoys and allies, 40% faster, and attacks 40% faster. Bosses shrug it off.
Tesla Coil (R)
One volt: the Thunder Glaive plants a tesla coil at your feet for 15s, up to 3 at once (another takes the oldest's place). While you have volts, every second each coil zaps the five enemies nearest it within 3 tiles (a small hit, Amped; a boss in reach 40% of one), and it passes the network's current to every Amped enemy within 3 tiles; a coil with an enemy that near burns a volt a second. Coils within 8 tiles of each other are joined by live fences, shocking as a cage's do, and the cage's posts fence into the nearest one. Out of volts they go dark (no zaps, no current, no fences) until you dash for more. When a streak of two links or more goes off, a bolt from the sky slams onto each coil, a light hit 2.5 tiles round it (wider the longer the streak).
Thrust
The Boreal Trident's first click: a quick poke down a line 8 tiles long and 3 wide, straight down your aim (short of a wall): a light hit, a little chill and two rime marks on everything on it. A moment later, as frost cracks run down the line, a spine of ice erupts out of the floor along it, a piece at a time over half a second with spurs off its sides and a great spike at the tip: the heavy hit, landing on each enemy as the spine reaches it. While the hurl is ready a press waits: let go and it pokes, hold on and the trident is drawn back for the throw; while the hurl comes back round a press pokes at once.
Tidecaller
Doesn't fight: every enemy near it attacks faster, a little on dry floor and far faster standing in water.
Time Lock
The Pendulum's field, 4s: a 7x7 with its corners cut, spread from where it lands over open floor (never round a wall). Time stops in it for everything but you: enemies can't move, act, cast or be moved, and their ailments don't run down (what they tick is stored with the rest). They take nothing: every hit you land on them is stored (its tally over them) and all of it lands at once when time starts again, kills and all. Shots, yours and theirs, stop at its edge and fly on after. A boss isn't frozen solid: its time runs 80% slower, but its damage is stored the same.
Twin Collapse
Click to place the anchor (right-click moves it). Every click after throws: the anchored star and its twin rush together and implode where you clicked, pulling enemies in and applying Hex to what they pass. The farther from the anchor, the harder and wider. In the full form each landing cashes in the Hex on what it lands on, as the big crunch does (nothing to cash, it Hexes it for the next).
Voidlets
Little void allies that draw enemy attention. Warriors chase and punch. Voidbeams lock on, then fire a wide beam that pierces and ricochets off walls. They stay with you until they fall.
Volts
The Thunder Glaive's fuel (24 in the tank): one for every enemy a pivot strike hits, and two back a link when a streak's boom goes off. A tesla coil costs 1. The network burns them: a volt a second while its current is reaching anyone, and a volt a second for each coil with an enemy within 3 tiles; with three coils at work a full tank lasts about 7s without a dash. At 0 the coils go dark and the network stops (with the fences that run to a coil) until you dash for more. The bar over you shows the tank, the part about to drain off it, and blinks amber when it runs low.
Wards
Orbiting shields. Each one eats one projectile, then shoves nearby enemies back.
Water
Supercharges the water enemies standing in it: Wave Riders charge sooner, Ripplerunners race, a Tidal Guard's hit pins you, a Tidecaller makes everything in it attack far faster (a little faster on dry floor). Fire dries it to fog.
